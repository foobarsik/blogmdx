### Translating "Love Tendencies" to Product

| Type           | In Relationships                                      | In Product                                                                 |
|----------------|--------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Romanticizer** | Believes in effortless, perfect love                  | 🔮 Waiting for the “perfect idea” / “viral product” — “If the product is great, it’ll take off on its own” |
| **Maximizer**    | Wants to pick the absolute best partner               | ⚙️ Constantly tweaking, comparing competitors, never launching — “It’s not ready yet”             |
| **Hesitater**    | “I need to become better before I start dating”       | 🐌 Afraid to start — “Need to level up more”, “Not ready for a side project yet” |

### Maximizer vs. Satisficer Behavior in Product Development

| Behavior            | **Maximizer**                                                                 | **Satisficer**                                               |
|---------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Features**         | Wants the perfect killer feature, builds an endless backlog, delays release  | MVP mindset: “Good enough to test”                            |
| **Tech Stack Choice**| Spends weeks choosing the stack — “What if there’s something better?”         | Uses what they know and moves forward                        |
| **UI/UX**            | “We want it beautiful and perfect from day one”                              | “Let it be usable — we’ll polish later”                      |
| **Product Decisions**| “We need to handle EVERY edge case and scenario”                             | “Let’s cover 80% — iterations will guide us”                 |
| **Release**          | “Not ready yet, just a bit more...”                                          | “Ship it and learn from feedback”                            |
